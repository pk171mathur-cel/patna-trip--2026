Places We Will Visit

Explore the most beautiful and educational destinations of Patna.

🛕 Mahavir Mandir

One of the most famous Hanuman temples in India, located near Patna Junction Railway Station. A place of faith, peace and devotion.

eco park

Rajdhani Vatika, better known as Ecological Park or Eco Park, is a park located on Strand Road in Patna, Bihar. This park was inaugurated in October 2011 by Nitish Kumar, the Chief Minister of Bihar, following an initiative of the Department of Environment and Forest, Bihar.

🌌 Patna Taramandal

One of India's largest planetariums where students will explore astronomy, planets and the universe through exciting shows.

🌉 Marine Drive

Enjoy the beautiful riverside road, fresh air and breathtaking views of the holy Ganga River.

🏛️ Golghar

A historical landmark of Patna offering a panoramic view of the city and an unforgettable experience.
